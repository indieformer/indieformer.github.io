You struck a deal with a dragon.

Twenty years is not survival. It is seasoning.

You traded twenty years of dinner service for the dragon's protection. The island does well under its watch, as long as the meals keep coming.

Every year the dragon wakes hungrier. You roll dice to work the island, fusing what you harvest into recipes and cooking it down in the Scorchpot, the pot you keep full or end up in. Get the meal wrong and you're smoke.
