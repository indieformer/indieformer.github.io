tHE HOUSE SEttLES EVERY DEBT oN A COIN.

You sold your soul for money. The buyer had antlers, stood behind a counter, and settles every debt on the toss of a coin. Now you're stuck in his purgatory, a place built out of Slavic folklore, and the only way out is to beat the house at its own game. On a coin. Good luck.

Every round gives you a score to hit and a set number of flips to hit it with. Pick a side, place your bet, flip. Wins pay out currency, and the currency buys Artifacts and Blessings that shift the odds, multiply your payouts and stack into each other. Miss the score and the run is over. No appeals.
