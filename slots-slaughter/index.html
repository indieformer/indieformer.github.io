Spin. Roll. Bleed.

Every fight starts at the slot machine. Spin to load your gear, then roll a d20 and watch it play out. Between fights you patch up at the Sanitarium and buy from the Merchant, stacking your deck for whatever the pit throws next.

Your items are the deck, and the machine decides what you play each turn. It's a slot machine and a combat game running at once.
